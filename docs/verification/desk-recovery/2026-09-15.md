# Markets and News display recovery

## Observed causes and fixes

- The News reader used full-width DECSTBM terminal scrolling inside a right-hand
  column. It now uses a fixed-height, independently repainted viewport. This
  prevents reader scrolling from moving Sources and headline rows.
- Help content height omitted the title gap. The shared scrollbar also accepted
  pending offsets beyond its range. Both viewport geometry and thumb position
  are now bounded, including after content shrinks.
- Markets linked its warning to help (`h`) instead of data status (`i`), and
  counted optional credentials as failures. Only required credentials and active
  source failures contribute to the warning.
- Article extraction joined separate body candidates and the reader preferred
  whichever response was longest. It now selects an article body, excludes page
  furniture, preserves basic Markdown structure, and prefers an extracted body
  over a longer feed excerpt. BBC and Guardian selectors have source fixtures.

## Read-only live checks

The selected numeric series from the running tester profile returned 199 quotes.
All 36 absent changes were Open-Meteo weather/air-quality forecasts: these do not
supply a previous forecast for the same valid period. They remain unavailable,
with an explicit explanation. Observation changes use the previous available
measurement, with actual dates when supplied; they are not labeled daily changes.
A separate sparse-history regression now preserves an older valid baseline
across more than 36 missing periods. BCB returned a partial timeout; other provider
groups were ready. No profile files or live processes were changed.

BBC, Guardian, Federal Reserve, EIA and Ars public article bodies were inspected.
BBC and Guardian now begin at the editorial body rather than surrounding metadata.
MercoPress returned a publisher database-error page and correctly produced no
article body. News retains the feed excerpt when extraction is unavailable.
Publisher layout changes remain possible; extraction does not bypass access gates.

## Regression coverage

- Real Ink rendering at 80×24 and 120×40: article scrolling, source navigation,
  pane-heading alignment, total frame height and preference for a shorter body.
- Shared help viewport: excessive scrolling and subsequent content shrink.
- Article parsing: sibling stories, split body sections, ads, hidden content,
  headings, lists and narrowly scoped publisher selectors.
- Sparse observation comparisons and honest forecast/zero-baseline explanations.
